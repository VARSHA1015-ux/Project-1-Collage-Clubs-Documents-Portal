01 / ABOUT THE CLUB

Building a Safer Digital Future

The Cyber Awareness Club is a student community focused on cybersecurity awareness, practical learning and responsible use of technology.

The club provides opportunities for students to explore online safety, ethical hacking, network security, data protection and emerging cybersecurity concepts.
