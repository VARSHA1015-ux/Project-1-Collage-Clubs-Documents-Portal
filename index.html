01 / ABOUT THE CLUB

Creating Cyber-Aware Digital Citizens.

The Cyber Awareness Club focuses on developing cybersecurity awareness, technical knowledge and responsible digital habits among students.

Practise Develop practical technical skills.

02 / DIRECTION

Vision & Mission

Our club aims to create a responsible, skilled and cyber-aware student community.

◈ Our Vision

To build a digitally responsible community where students understand cybersecurity risks and confidently adopt safe digital practices.

◈ Our Mission

To provide students with awareness programs, practical learning opportunities, technical activities and collaborative projects related to cybersecurity.
