ABOUT THE CLUB

Building a Cyber-Aware Community

The Cyber Awareness Club is a student community dedicated to creating awareness and developing practical knowledge about cybersecurity.

The club provides students with opportunities to learn about online safety, ethical hacking, network security, data protection, emerging cyber threats and digital privacy.

Through workshops, seminars, quizzes, competitions and practical activities, students can improve their technical knowledge and responsible use of technology.

🎯

Vision

To build a community of skilled and responsible students who can understand, prevent, and respond to cybersecurity threats.

🚀

Mission

Promote cybersecurity awareness, develop practical skills, encourage ethical hacking and conduct workshops, seminars, quizzes and competitions.
