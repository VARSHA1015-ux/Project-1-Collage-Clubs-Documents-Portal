01 / ABOUT THE CLUB

BUILDING A SAFER DIGITAL WORLD.

The Cyber Awareness Club is a student-centered technical community dedicated to building cybersecurity knowledge, digital responsibility and practical technical skills.

Through workshops, awareness campaigns, projects and technical events, the club helps students understand modern cyber threats and develop safer digital habits.
