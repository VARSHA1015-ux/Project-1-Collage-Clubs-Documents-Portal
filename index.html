01 / ABOUT

Building a safer digital generation.

The Cyber Awareness Club is a student-driven technical community focused on cybersecurity, digital safety, awareness, and responsible technology usage.

Awareness Promoting safe and responsible digital habits.
Innovation Encouraging practical cybersecurity projects.
Learning Creating opportunities for technical growth.
Community Connecting students through cybersecurity.
02 / DIRECTION

Vision & Mission

VISION

To create a digitally aware student community capable of understanding cyber risks and responding to them responsibly.

MISSION

To provide cybersecurity awareness, practical learning opportunities, workshops, projects and technical events for students.
