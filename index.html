WHO WE ARE

ABOUT THE CLUB

Building Cyber-Aware Minds

The Cyber Awareness Club is a student community focused on cybersecurity awareness and practical skills.

We cover online safety, ethical hacking, network security and data protection.

Our goal is to create skilled and responsible students who can prevent and respond to cyber threats.
