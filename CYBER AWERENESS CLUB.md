# 🔐 CYBER SECURITY CLUB

> **Learn • Secure • Protect**

---

## 📌 About the Club

The **Cyber Security Club** is a student community dedicated to creating awareness and developing practical knowledge about cybersecurity.

The club provides students with opportunities to learn about:

- 🔐 Online Safety
- 💻 Ethical Hacking
- 🌐 Network Security
- 🛡️ Data Protection
- ⚠️ Emerging Cyber Threats
- 🔒 Digital Privacy
- 🚨 Cybercrime Awareness

---

## 🎯 Vision

> To build a community of **skilled and responsible students** who can understand, prevent, and respond to cybersecurity threats.

---

## 🚀 Mission

- 💠 Promote cybersecurity awareness among students.
- 💠 Develop practical cybersecurity skills.
- 💠 Encourage ethical hacking and responsible security practices.
- 💠 Conduct workshops, seminars, quizzes, and competitions.
- 💠 Introduce students to current cybersecurity trends and threats.
- 💠 Encourage teamwork, problem-solving, and continuous learning.

---

## 🛠️ Main Activities

- 🔐 Cybersecurity Awareness Programs
- 💻 Ethical Hacking Workshops
- 🏆 Capture The Flag (CTF) Competitions
- 🧠 Cybersecurity Quizzes
- 🎤 Guest Lectures and Seminars
- 🌐 Network Security Demonstrations
- 🛡️ Cyber Safety Campaigns
- 🚀 Cybersecurity Projects and Challenges

## 🎯 Objectives

1. Improve students' understanding of cybersecurity.

3. Provide hands-on learning opportunities.
