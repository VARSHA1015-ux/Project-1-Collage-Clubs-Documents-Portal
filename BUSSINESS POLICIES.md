# 📋Club Operational Policies & Governance Framework

This document outlines the operational policies, code of conduct, and administrative guidelines governing all club activities, repository management, and member interactions.

---

## 1. Code of Conduct

* **Inclusivity:** All activities, events, and online communications must foster an inclusive, welcoming, and respectful environment for members of all backgrounds.
* **Professionalism:** Committee and club members must maintain professional communication across official channels (Slack, WhatsApp, GitHub, and email).
* **Academic Integrity:** All work produced, code submitted, and technical presentations created under the club's banner must adhere to the institution's academic integrity standards.

---

## 2. GitHub Governance & Codebase Policy

* **Branching Model:** Direct pushes to the `main` branch are strictly prohibited. All changes must be introduced via dedicated feature branches (`feature/<topic>` or `fix/<topic>`).
* **Pull Request (PR) Approval:** Every PR requires at least one peer review approval from a Core Executive Committee member before merging.
* **Documentation Standard:** All major events, financial transactions, and official meetings must be documented using the repository's standardized Markdown templates within 48 hours of occurrence.

---

## 3. Financial & Budgeting Policy

* **Expense Authorization:** Any expense exceeding ₹1,000 must receive prior written approval from both the Treasurer and the Faculty Advisor.
* **Reimbursement Protocol:** Original itemized bills and receipts must be scanned and uploaded to the repository's `budgets/` directory within 7 days of purchase.
* **Sponsorship Rules:** Sponsorship agreements must be reviewed by the Public Relations Lead and approved by the Faculty Advisor.

---

## 4. Attendance & Activity Policy

* **Meeting Attendance:** Committee members are required to attend a minimum of 85% of scheduled meetings per semester.
* **Certificate Eligibility:** Certificates for workshops and events will only be issued to participants who complete at least 80% of the session duration and submit required deliverables.
* **Handover Protocol:** Outgoing executive board members must conduct a formal knowledge transfer session and hand over repository administrative privileges at the end of their tenure.
